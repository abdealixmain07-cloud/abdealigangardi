# White portfolio theme

## Goal
Shift the portfolio from charcoal to a polished white theme without changing its content or interactions.

## Changes
- Replace the global dark palette with warm white surfaces, charcoal text, soft gray borders, and restrained gold accents.
- Remove the forced dark appearance and update glass panels, shadows, and focus states for clear contrast on white.
- Rebalance the Dubai skyline overlay so it remains visible while hero text stays readable.
- Update any dark-only borders and hover treatments across navigation, projects, timeline, tools, and contact areas.
- Verify the complete page on desktop and mobile, including portrait loading, expandable projects, links, and console health.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing semantic color tokens and remap their values in the global stylesheet.
- Preserve the current responsive layout, animations, reduced-motion behavior, SEO metadata, and image-loading logic.
